# Level 8 · Strength Endurance
Aim: The full front lever, the straddle planche, the V-sit and deficit handstand push-ups join, the full back lever climbs to 10 s and one-arm pull-up negatives begin. Doses climb faster now. Miss an exam and repeat the block: that's how this level works.
Entry: passed the Level 7 test (straddle_front 4s, full_back 4s, full_flag 3s/side, shrimp_squat 2/side, strict_muscleup 3, pullup 10, one_arm_pushup 2/side)

Rotation: 1 front_onearm · 2 legs_core · 3 planche_push · 4 handstand_press · 5 back_flag · 6 battle · 7 recovery
