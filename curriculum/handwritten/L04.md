# Level 4 · Skill Control
Aim: Turn the tuck shapes into the first real skills: the tuck planche, the freestanding handstand, the advanced-tuck levers and the first wall handstand push-up.
Entry: passed the Level 3 test (tuck_back_hold 5s, tuck_front_hold 10s, frog_stand 20s, pullup 5, pseudo_planche_pushup 5, pushup 13, bent_dragon_negative 3)

Rotation: 1 front_pull · 2 legs_core · 3 planche_push · 4 handstand_press · 5 back_flag · 6 basics · 7 recovery (handstand on legs, press, bar and basics days; none on the front-lever and planche days; light weeks 011–012, 032–033, 053–054, 074–075; deload weeks 050–054 and 092–097)
Blocks: 001–013 Pistols & Back Lever · 014–034 Toes-to-Bar & Flag · 035–055 First Tuck Planche · 056–076 First Freestanding · 077–100 Level Test
