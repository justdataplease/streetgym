# Level 7 · Full Shapes
Aim: The straddle front lever, the first full back lever and full flag, one-arm push-ups, shrimp squats and one-arm pull-up groundwork. Full shapes take most adults years: until then, train the rung below.
Entry: passed the Level 6 test (freestanding_handstand 24s, advanced_tuck_planche 5s, one_leg_front 7s/side, straddle_back 6s, lsit 13s, strict_muscleup 2, pullup 9)
Exit (level test 100): straddle_front 4s, full_back 4s, full_flag 3s/side, shrimp_squat 2/side, strict_muscleup 3, pullup 10, one_arm_pushup 2/side (freestanding_handstand 27s at 034 and advanced_tuck_planche 6s at 076 are tested in the block exams)
Rotation: 1 front_pull · 2 legs_core · 3 planche_push · 4 handstand_press · 5 back_flag · 6 basics · 7 recovery
Blocks: 001–013 Full Shapes, block 1 · 014–034 Full Shapes, block 2 · 035–055 Full Shapes, block 3 · 056–076 Full Shapes, block 4 · 077–100 Full Shapes, block 5
