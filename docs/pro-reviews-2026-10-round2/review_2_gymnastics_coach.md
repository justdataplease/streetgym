# Street Gymnastics: can an ordinary adult climb every skill ladder? (gymnastics coach review)

**Scope.** I read all 700 classes in `classes_digest.txt`: MAIN, PRACTICE and the movement library, with cooldowns checked in `curriculum_v2.json`. I traced each ladder with scripts in `pro3\r2\`: `parse.py`, `trace.py` (first use, timelines, fallbacks), `gates.py`, `fallback2.py`, `gaps.py`, `jumps.py`, `load.py`, `planche_pairs.py` and `sets.py`/`daysets.py` for set caps.

**Conventions.**
- One class is one day, so class L3:082 is day 282.
- "Gate met" means an earlier class prescribes at least the dose named in the movement's "Start when" text.
- When a step lists no easier rung, the app falls back to the library `regression_id` (`CurriculumJsonParser.kt:185-186`). So practice steps such as `handstand_balance` do have a fallback.
- Authentic MAIN steps (the quoted names) have no easier rung at all.

**Realism note.** For an adult starting from zero, most people will not reach the top rungs on schedule: full levers around day 640, the full flag, straddle planche and one-arm negatives. That is acceptable only because every rung has a fallback for the same sets. So I weight the fallback chain as heavily as the gates.

## 3. What is already good (keep it)

- **Practice comes before MAIN in every class.** Handstand and flag skill work is never done after pressing in the same session.
- **Wrist and shoulder preparation.**
  - All 490 classes with loaded wrist extension have `wrist_rocks` or `wrist_circles` in the warm-up.
  - Cooldowns include `forearm_stretch` (293 classes), `lat_stretch` (328) and `hamstring_stretch` (163).
- **The handstand base is textbook.**
  - Box-pike hold from L1:071, then chest-to-wall from L2:071 at 1x10s, rising to 3x40s by L4:036.
  - The cartwheel exit starts 35 classes before toe pulls, and toe pulls run for 100 classes before balance attempts.
  - The toe-pull gate is met in 25 sessions before first use, and the balance gate in 50.
- **The HSPU ladder has every intermediate rung, and every gate is met.**
  - Pike push-up (L1:078), elevated pike (L4:059), negatives (L5:006; 5-rep sets at L5:090 and L5:097), wall HSPU (L6:007), 3x5 (L7:014 and L7:021), deficit (L7:035), 3x3 (L7:042 and L7:049), freestanding negative (L7:063).
  - One to two sessions a week, never on consecutive days.
- **The L-sit is never skipped.**
  - Tuck L-sit runs from L1:085 (10 s) to L3:096, at 15–25 s from L2:082 on.
  - One-leg from L4:001 (6 to 10 s/side), full L-sit from L5:001 (6 to 20 s).
- **Dragon flag.**
  - Bent negatives (L3:026), then one-leg negatives (L4:009, kept at 1–2x1/side for 9 weeks), then full negatives (L5:002, 3x2 five times), then reps (L5:058). Every gate is met.
- **Lever spacing.**
  - Tuck, advanced-tuck, one-leg and straddle front levers are 60–130 classes apart, and the 6-week rule is honoured (L3:035 to L4:092).
  - The one-leg and straddle back-lever gates are met.
  - German hang rises steadily from 10 to 30 s.
- **Muscle-up.**
  - Low-bar transition (L1:045), pullover (L2:031), jump and negative muscle-ups (L2:073), band (L2:076), strict (L3:063).
  - The strict gate is met: authentic pull-up 1x12 at L3:013, `chest_to_bar` 3x5 at L3:060, `straight_bar_dip` 10+ from L3:028.
  - The band fallback is in the class throughout.
- **Early flag ladder.**
  - Feet-grounded support runs 170 classes before the foot-assisted tuck.
  - The tuck-flag and straddle-flag gates are met in 19 and 9 sessions.
- **Fallbacks.** Except the three pairs in m3, every listed easier rung has been practised before it is shown.
- **Recovery and set caps.**
  - Every full week has at least one day at RPE ≤4 (d4 from L4 on), plus a d7 mobility day in L4–L5.
  - There is a deload every 3–4 weeks.
  - My count finds no class above its set cap.
- **Gaps.** No skill disappears and returns at a harder rung, except where an authentic class carried it in between. For example, the back lever from L2:096 to L3:029 is bridged by authentic "lower to one-third back lever" in L3:003–L3:017.
