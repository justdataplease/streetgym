# Street Gymnastics: statics and bar-skill review (outside coach)

Scope: all 700 classes in `classes_digest.txt`, MAIN + PRACTICE as the app prescribes them, plus the movement library gates. I traced every rung with scripts in `scratchpad\pro3\r1\` (`parse.py`, `trace.py` for dose timelines and gaps, `gates.py` for every "Start when" gate, `checks.py` for fallbacks, caps and stacking, `weekly.py` for day-to-day pulling load, `totals.py` for set counts). Notation: `L6:051 full_front 1x3s` = class, movement, sets x dose.

## 2. Findings

Severity:
- **Blocker:** a typical user cannot do the step as written and has no listed easier rung.
- **Major:** the rung or dose arrives before its own gate or preparation, or a combination meaningfully raises tendon risk on a main skill.
- **Minor:** a consistency or efficiency problem.

Every fix keeps the sets inside the caps. I checked the totals with `totals.py`, and most target classes are already at their cap, so most fixes are swaps.

## 3. What is already good (keep it)

- **Complete ladders in the right order** for every skill. No intermediate rung is missing: tuck → advanced tuck → one-leg → straddle → full for both levers; low-bar transition → pullover → jump/negative/band → strict for the MU; grounded support → foot-assisted tuck → tuck → straddle → full for the flag; pike → elevated pike → wall negative → wall → deficit → freestanding negative for the HSPU.
- **Patient middle phases with gates met by wide margins:**
  - About 150 classes of advanced-tuck FL before the one-leg (the six-week rule is easily met).
  - About 200 classes of foot-assisted tuck flag (L3:075–L5:073).
  - About 300 classes of wall handstand before balance work.
  - 19 sessions of 3x8s assisted flags before the free tuck flag; 9 sessions of 3x5s tuck before the straddle flag.
