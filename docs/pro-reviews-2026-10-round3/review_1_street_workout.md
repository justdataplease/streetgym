# Review 1: bar and static skills (round 3)

Reviewer lens: street-workout head coach (levers, planche, flag, strict muscle-up, one-arm pull-up).

Method: I parsed all 700 classes and the library from `classes_digest.txt`. Scripts are in `round3\r1\`:
- `parse.py`, `trace.py`: rung timelines and gaps;
- `gates.py`: all 34 "Start when" gates;
- `weekly.py`: frequency and straight-arm runs;
- `caps.py`: set caps.

I also read the raw JSON to confirm which original-source steps have a `movement_id`, and so an easier rung.

Facts the checks established:
- All 700 classes are within the set caps.
- The longest run of RPE ≥7 days is 3.
- Every gate is prescribed before its rung starts. The full levers are checked in the same class.
- 350 of the 379 original-source steps have no `movement_id`, so they show no easier rung.

### The whole journey: what a typical adult reaches by L7, and where they stall

Typical L7 outcomes, if they train daily and use the fallbacks (my coaching estimate from the doses):
- **Reached by most:** strict muscle-up in sets of 3–5 (L6–L7); 10–20 s freestanding handstand; brief full back lever (many); dragon flag; typewriter and assisted one-arm pull-ups.
- **Reached by a minority:** full front lever (most finish at one-leg or straddle); full human flag (most finish at tuck or straddle); freestanding HSPU negatives (most finish at wall HSPU); straddle planche (most finish at tuck or advanced tuck).

Where most adults stall, and whether the fallbacks cover it:
1. **First pull-up** (L1:094 2x1, then L2:001 3x4). Many women and heavier adults will be on `assisted_pullup` through L2. The fallback covers it; it only pushes the muscle-up gate out by months. Acceptable.
2. **Front lever, advanced tuck to one-leg (L5–L7).** This is the main plateau. L5 covers it, because advanced tuck is in most FL classes. L6–L7 do not (M5).
3. **Human flag at tuck (L6–L7).** Not covered: there is no rung between tuck and straddle (M4).
4. **HSPU at wall negatives or 1–3 reps (L6–L7).** The fallback chain is one rung deep and the plateau rung leaves L7 (M6).
5. **Planche at tuck.** Covered only through the bent-arm frog stand in L5 (m1).

The structural limit is that every step lists only one easier rung. L7 keeps two rungs below the target in the class (good: L7:030, L7:033). Any lifter three rungs behind loses their working dose. The fixes below put the plateau rung back in for FL, flag and HSPU without exceeding any cap.

Week structure is sound: no class over cap, at most three RPE ≥7 days in a row, a recovery day every week and a deload every fourth week. In L7, straight-arm skill work falls on d5, d6, d1 and d2 (four of five days). That is acceptable at these short holds.

## 3. Keep (do not change)

- **Gate discipline.** All 34 "Start when" gates are prescribed before the rung starts (gates.py). Each advanced rung enters as a single test set with its easier rung beside it, e.g. the muscle-up check at L3:065 and the one-leg front gate at L4:092.
- **Feet-supported back-lever ladder.** Partial skin-the-cat → German hang → feet-assisted tuck entry → holds. Every BL day from L3:061 to L7:099 opens the shoulders first: `german_hang` in L4, L5 and L7, `skin_cat_partial` in L6. It is the right way to look after the biceps tendon.
- **Handstand ladder.** A daily practice block from L1:001. Chest-to-wall 3x40s before toe pulls, the cartwheel exit 135 classes before balance, kick-ups before balance, then daily balance windows from L5:071.
- **L7 same-class fallbacks.** The target, the straddle and the one-leg rung in one class (L7:030, L7:033, L7:058). M5 and M4 only add the rung below.
- **Spacing of the lever rungs.** 106–160 classes between front-lever rungs, the six-week clause on `one_leg_front`, and an advanced tuck held for 157 classes. Don't compress these.
- **Load management.** Deload every fourth week, a recovery day every week, at most three RPE ≥7 days in a row, and no class over its set cap in any of the 700.
- **Projection swaps already made.** Pull-Up Negative from L1:033, Small Hollow-to-Arch Swing from L1:031, Band MU, Tuck FL Hold and Feet-Assisted Low-Bar Transition. B1, M1, M2 and m6 extend the same mechanism.
- **Dragon-flag ladder.** Bent → one-leg → full negatives → dragon flag, each gated on 3x2–3x3.
