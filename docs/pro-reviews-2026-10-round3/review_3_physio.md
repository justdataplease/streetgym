# Review 3: can an untrained adult achieve every progression? (physio and S&C view)

**Scope:** all 700 classes in `classes_digest.txt`, plus the projected `training_content` of the 90 original-source classes in `curriculum_v2.json`. The digest prints compound sets without their element names, so I read those names from the JSON. `app/src/main/assets/curriculum.json` is byte-identical to that file.

**Tracing scripts** are in `round3/r3/`:
- `parse.py`, `compact.py`: parse the digest and build per-level views.
- `timeline.py`: dose timeline of each movement.
- `authsets.py`: original-source set names.
- `load.py`, `weekly.py`, `rawpull.py`: weekly load by pattern.
- `rpe.py`: RPE runs and deload spacing.
- `gaps.py`: movements that vanish and return.
- `fallbacks.py`: whether each fallback rung was practised.
- `elbow.py`: consecutive elbow-loaded days.

**"Strict pull reps"** means strict pull-ups, chin-ups, chest-to-bar, muscle-ups (MU) and negatives, archer, typewriter and one-arm reps (one-arm and archer work counted per side). Assisted and foot-supported reps are not counted.

**Abbreviations:** FL = front lever, BL = back lever, MU = muscle-up, C2B = chest-to-bar, SB = straight-bar, HSPU = handstand push-up, HLR = hanging leg raise, T2B = toes-to-bar, SL-RDL = single-leg Romanian deadlift, OAP = one-arm pull-up.

## 3. Keep (do not change)

- **Gate chain.** Every library "Start when" gate is prescribed before its rung:
  - Front lever: tuck 3x10s at L3:027, then advanced tuck at L3:035; advanced tuck 3x10s at L4:085, then one-leg at L4:092, more than 6 weeks after L3:035; one-leg 3x8s/side at L5:092, then straddle at L6:024.
  - Muscle-up: C2B 3x5 at L3:060 and pull-up 2x10 at L3:063, then the MU check at L3:065.
  - Pistol: assisted 3x8/side at L4:041, then pistols at L4:058.
  - Planche: lean 3x20s at L3:064, then tuck at L4:017.
  - HSPU: wall negative 3x5 at L5:090, then wall HSPU at L6:007, 3x5 at L7:014, then deficit at L7:035.
  - One-arm: archer 3x5/side at L7:010, then typewriter at L7:021; typewriter 3x3/side at L7:035, then assisted one-arm at L7:063; assisted one-arm 3x3/side at L7:070, then negatives at L7:084.
  - Flag: grounded 3x15s at L3:037, then assisted at L3:075; assisted 3x8s at L5:002, then tuck at L5:073; tuck 3x5s at L6:037, then straddle at L6:072; straddle 3x5s at L7:036, then full at L7:041.
- **Handstand ladder.** Daily micro-doses: wall from L2:071, exit from L4:036, toe pull from L4:071, kick-up, then balance from L5:071. There are no balance attempts before the exit is practised.
- **Readiness checks.** Full levers, the straddle planche, the full flag and the Nordic curl start as single readiness checks. In L7 the fallback rung stays in every class: one_leg_front 1x8s/side on every FL day (L7:002-093), one_leg_back on every BL day, and tuck plus straddle flag alongside the full flag.
- **Deloads inside L6 and L7** come every 3-4 weeks (L6:015-021, 036-041, 057-062; L7:022-028, 050-056, 078-084).
- **L4 and L5** keep two recovery days a week (d4 and d7).
- **Caps** are respected everywhere: maximum sets per class are 14, 16, 18, 18, 20, 20, 20 for L1-L7, with no exercise over 3 sets (L1-L2) or 4 (L3 on).
- **Projection swaps already made** must stay: standalone "Muscle Up" becomes band_muscleup, behind-the-neck pulls become front pull-ups, and former max sets are capped at 8.
- **Slow ladders:** Nordic negatives (2x3 at L4:058 to 3x8 at L7:090, weekly) and the dragon flag ladder are gradual.

**Verdict on the journey.** The library ladders (the 610 non-original classes) are paced realistically for the basics: pull-ups, dips, push-ups, pistols, L-sit, wall HSPU, handstand and back lever. They are deliberately ambitious for front lever, planche, flag and one-arm work. That ambition is acceptable because every hard step is a gated ceiling with a practised fallback in the same class.

Most people will plateau at:
- strict MU in L4-L5,
- advanced-tuck or one-leg front lever in L6-L7,
- tuck or advanced-tuck planche,
- tuck or straddle flag.

All of these are covered by the fallbacks.

The real achievability failures are not in the library. They are:
- the three original-source blocks (B1, B2, M1, M2), which demand more than the surrounding library has built and list no easier rung;
- the three load cliffs at L2:001, L3:001-020 and L6:001 (M3, B1, M4);
- the missing deloads across level ends (M6, M7).

With B1-M7 applied, I would expect a consistent adult who uses the rungs to reach L7 without the program forcing a jump they were not prepared for.
